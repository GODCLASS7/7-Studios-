## 3. Hard rules (all binding)

1. House style only: near-black stage, warm off-white type, one gold accent (src/lib/tokens.ts). No other colours.
2. Every cut, word landing and accent sits on the 120 BPM grid in src/videos/Teaser/beats.ts (beat = 0.5 s). Audio and picture are built from the same numbers.
3. No AI footage in this cut: the plan was Remotion-only (0 generation credits). Any regenerated shot needs a new storyboard version and an approved quote.
4. Headlines break into balanced lines with no orphan word, in every format; portrait lines are set explicitly where needed.
5. The 9:16 cut-down starts on the title (no cold open), keeps every later scene the same length, and trims the audio by 4.0 s so the final hit still lands on the end card.

**Studio rules (always apply)**
- Every text, logo, number and UI element is set in Remotion, never rendered by an AI model.
- All motion comes from `useCurrentFrame()`; timing is in seconds × fps; no CSS transitions, timers or `Math.random()`.
- Fonts are bundled locally and loaded with `@remotion/fonts`; nothing loads from the network at render time.
- No paid generation without an approved quote, and no skipped approval gates (storyboard, style frames, review cut).
- No real-person likeness without consent; no generated third-party logos or trademarks.

## 7. Visual system

**Palette** (`src/lib/tokens.ts`)
| Token | Hex |
|---|---|
| bg | `#0B0B0D` |
| surface | `#17171B` |
| text | `#F4EFE6` |
| muted | `#9A958C` |
| accent | `#E8B44A` |
| line | `#2A2A30` |
| paper | `#F7F5F0` |
| ink | `#1C1C1F` |

**Type scale** (px at the 1080p authoring size; 4K renders at 2×): hero 140 · h1 96 · h2 64 · h3 44 · body 32 · caption 24

**Springs:** smooth `{ damping: 200 }` · snappy `{ damping: 20, stiffness: 200 }` · punch `{ damping: 12, stiffness: 180 }`

- Stage: radial gradient #1F1D1A → #0B0B0D, film look (`<Grade look="film">`), vignette 0.3–0.45, animated grain 5–7% over everything.
- Headlines: Inter 900, tight leading; the key word in gold ("speed", "format.").
- Overlines and sublines: Inter 600, letter-spacing 0.2–0.32em, uppercase, muted #9A958C.
- Pills and badges: gold #E8B44A fill, near-black text, fully rounded.

**Motion vocabulary**
| Move | How it's built |
|---|---|
| Beam sweep | A 100° linear-gradient band (screen blend) travels −35% → 135% of the width over 0.3–3.3 s with ease.inOut; each screen's glow = exp(−((x−beam)/0.22w)²). |
| Kick pulse | On the 3.5 s kick: screen borders turn gold and brightness spikes, decaying as exp(−frames/5); a 12% gold screen-blend flash. |
| Title slam | KineticTitle with `landOn` = the beat frames; each word springs (snappy) 0.45 s before it lands; the gold rule draws over 0.7 s after the last word. |
| Stamp | FOR APPROVAL (orange) fades in at 0.4 s; APPROVED (green) punches in at 9.0 s, scale 1.7 → 1 with the punch spring, rotated −8°. |
| Wipe reveal | clip-path inset reveals the frozen rendered frame (frame 75 of DemoTitle) over the sketch, 2.5–3.5 s, with a glowing gold edge. |
| Tile pop | Format tiles land on beats 12.5–14.5 s: translateY 60 → 0 and scale 0.7 → 1 (snappy), 8 frames of travel. |
| Wordmark punch | On the 16 s hit: letter-spacing 0.45 → 0.06em and scale 1.15 → 1, gold flash 0.5 → 0 over 8 frames, slow pull-out 1.08 → 1, fade to black in the last 0.6 s. |

## 14. QA checklist

- ☐ `npm run lint` passes (ESLint + TypeScript).
- ☐ Every final passes `npm run qc`: resolution, fps, exact duration, BT.709, loudness and true peak.
- ☐ Hierarchy: one focal point per frame; text readable at phone size; contrast ≥ 4.5:1 for body text.
- ☐ Safe areas: nothing important within 5% of the edges; on 9:16, bottom 20% and right 12% clear.
- ☐ No orphan words on headlines; brand palette only; spelling and numbers match the brief.
- ☐ Every word lands on its beat (check stills at 4.25, 4.5 … 5.5 s).
- ☐ No near-black frame at the 12 s cut other than the sparse heading frame (blackdetect may flag 12.00–12.27 s; that is expected).
- ☐ The 9:16 heading reads "One brief." / "Every format."; no orphan words anywhere.

**Pitfalls found during production**
- `<Freeze>` inside a single-frame `<Still>` shows frame 0. Land animations in the past (negative `landOn`) instead.
- Google Fonts can't load in the cloud container's Chromium (proxy certificate). Fonts are bundled locally.
- Remotion's bundled ffmpeg lacks blackdetect, lut3d, tile and fps. QC and contact sheets use a system ffmpeg.
- blackdetect flags sparse dark frames (a few words on near-black) as black; review the flagged frames before acting.
- In portrait, flex-wrapped headlines broke as "One brief. Every / format."; set the lines explicitly.
