- Record preferences, not one-offs. "Captions smaller and lower on talking heads" is a preference; "remove the second take at 0:12" is not.
- Say where it applies (which recipe, format or series) and which job it came from.
- When a note contradicts an older one, replace the old one, and say so in the entry.
- A preference that holds across clients belongs in a recipe in `SKILL.md`; move it there.

Format: `- <preference> (applies to: <recipe/format/series>; from <job id>, <date>)`
